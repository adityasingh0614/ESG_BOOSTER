Sustainability reporting is no longer optional. Being ready for it can be your edge.

With BRSR disclosures and their assurance now part of the reporting cycle for listed companies and their value chains, organisations need people who understand both the framework and the work behind it.

The ESG Booster Workshop brings academics from GGSIPU’s University School of Environment Management together with practitioners who prepare, review and verify ESG disclosures for leading companies. You’ll spend the day on what actually happens in practice: how BRSR is put together, what assurance providers check, and how organisations have turned sustainability commitments into results.

After two well-attended sessions, this third edition is open to students and working professionals alike, with plenty of time to ask questions and meet people working in the field.

Who should attend

  • EHS, ESG and sustainability professionals
  • Compliance, CSR, finance and reporting teams working on BRSR
  • Consultants and auditors moving into ESG assurance
  • Students and researchers in environment, management and law

What you’ll learn

Four focus areas, taught by people who work with ESG disclosures every day.

Mastering BRSR

How the Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report is structured, what each section asks for, and how organisations gather the data behind it.

Assurance and verification of ESG disclosures

What assurance providers look for, the common gaps they find, and how to keep your ESG data audit-ready through the year.

Success stories: ESG in action

Real examples of organisations that moved beyond compliance, and what made their sustainability programmes work.

Experience sharing by ESG experts

Open discussion with practitioners on careers, client expectations and the questions you bring along.
