About the event

A gathering of people shaping ESG in India.

The ESG Booster Workshop brings together people working across ESG, sustainability, EHS, compliance, finance, consulting and academia. It is a space to meet, exchange perspectives and understand what is changing across the ESG landscape in India.

The day is built around panel discussions, short expert conversations, practical stories and open networking. Topics such as BRSR and ESG assurance will be touched upon through discussions and experience-sharing. The focus is on connecting people and sharing perspectives, rather than detailed classroom-style training.

Who you’ll meet

  • ESG, EHS and sustainability leaders from industry
  • Consultants, auditors and assurance professionals
  • Compliance, CSR, finance and reporting teams
  • Academics, researchers and students interested in ESG
  • Professionals exploring new roles, partnerships and opportunities in the ESG ecosystem

What you’ll take away

The workshop is designed to give participants more than a set of presentations. By the end of the day, you should have:

New connections with people working across industry, consulting and academia.

Different perspectives on ESG developments and practical challenges in India.

Ideas and examples you can take back to your organisation or professional work.

Opportunities to explore collaboration, partnerships and career connections.

A clearer sense of the conversations and opportunities emerging across the ESG sector.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a training programme?
No. The ESG Booster Workshop is a one-day gathering built around panel discussions, short expert conversations, experience-sharing and networking. BRSR and ESG assurance will be touched upon in discussions, but the event is not a detailed classroom training programme.
Who can attend?
The workshop is open to students, working professionals, ESG and sustainability practitioners, EHS professionals, consultants, auditors, assurance experts, compliance teams, academics and researchers.
Do I need prior experience in ESG?
No. The conversations are intended to be practical and accessible to people who are already working in ESG as well as those who are exploring the field.
What will happen during the day?
The programme includes panel discussions, short expert conversations, real-world experience-sharing, audience questions and networking breaks. The detailed agenda will be shared on this page and with registered participants.
Who are the speakers?
The confirmed speakers are listed on this page. Additional speakers and panellists will be announced as their participation is confirmed.
What is included in the registration fee?
Tea and coffee during networking breaks, lunch followed by high tea, and a certificate of participation.
